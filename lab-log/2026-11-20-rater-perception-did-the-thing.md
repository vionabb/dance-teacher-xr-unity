# What the CHI25 raters were judging: "did the thing"

## Viona's account (verbatim, 2026-11-20)

This was a comment on the human-ceiling finding in
[2026-11-20-metric-rater-gap-drivers.md](2026-11-20-metric-rater-gap-drivers.md),
where 39% of queue segments are rated 3/3 by all three raters:

> i feel like the implicit task human raters were doing was seeing if people "did
> the thing". if they saw the participants performing the key semantic motions,
> it counted as 3/3. they weren't comparing joint angles or even timing very
> closely. i wonder how we could capture this "did we recognize an effort to do
> some semantic movement, at about the right time" type of perception. i'm
> guessing that human raters deducted points when they saw someone forget to do a
> recognizable move entirely, or do it in a quite-noticably wrong way. the human
> raters certainly were not calibrating their ratings based on expert
> performances -- a 3/3 was very much "good for a novice having tried to learn a
> tiktok dance for 12 minutes".

## Status

These are Viona's hypotheses about rater perception, not tested findings. She
asked for them to be recorded and for the coordinator to suggest follow-ups.

## How it relates to existing evidence

Agent notes. Where an item is inferred, it says so.

- **Ceiling.** Observed: 39% of segments are rated 5 and make up 88% of the
  harshest decile of metric–rater gaps. This fits a coarse "did they do it"
  rating that a graded joint-angle metric cannot match at the top.
- **Within-clip agreement.** Observed: Qijia2D orders about 73% of
  differently rated same-clip pairs the way the raters do. Inferred: much of
  that agreement may come from the clear failures, where a move was skipped or
  done very wrong, rather than from fine gradations.
- **Timing.** Observed: rescoring at each segment's best time shift (≤0.5 s)
  did not improve agreement. This is consistent with "at about the right time"
  rather than frame-exact timing.
- **Recognizable moves.** This connects to stage 2 of
  `lab-log/2026-11-06-rating-relevant-time-spans.md` (on PR #414's branch):
  recognizable moves versus coincidental motion, with move spans that Viona
  marks before seeing any results.
